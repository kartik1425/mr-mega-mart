# Privacy Policy — MR Mega Mart

**Last Updated:** August 10, 2026

Welcome to **MR Mega Mart**. Your privacy and data security are paramount to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is collected, used, and protected within the MR Mega Mart application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide a seamless grocery shopping experience, MR Mega Mart collects:
- **Account Information:** Full Name, Email Address, and encrypted credentials for authentication.
- **Delivery Addresses:** Street Name, Apartment/Suite, City, Postal Code, and Phone Number for order fulfillment.
- **Cart & Order History:** Selected grocery items, quantities, transaction history, and saved preferences.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is used strictly for internal operational purposes:
- Authenticating user accounts securely via JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
- Processing grocery orders and delivering items to your specified address.
- Personalizing product search recommendations and deal promotions.
- Maintaining customer support and account security.

---

## 3. Data Protection & Security
- **No Third-Party Selling:** MR Mega Mart strictly DOES NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers.
- **Encryption:** Passwords are hashed securely using industry-standard `bcrypt`.
- **Payment Processing:** Payment processing occurs in staging mode via `NullPaymentProvider`. No credit card numbers or banking credentials are saved on our servers.

---

## 4. Account Management & Rights
You have full control over your data:
- You may view and edit your profile details at any time in the **Account Settings**.
- You can manage saved delivery addresses directly from **My Addresses**.
- To request complete account deletion, contact our support team.
